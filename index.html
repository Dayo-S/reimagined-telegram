Banned Book Club

Reading forbidden stories, discussing freedom

Welcome

We are a group of students who read banned books — stories that have been challenged, restricted, or removed from libraries and classrooms. Each meeting we explore the themes that make these books targets, talk about why censorship matters, and share what the stories give us.

Core Themes

  • Censorship & Freedom — How books become “banned” and what we lose when stories are silenced.
  • Diversity & Representation — Voices that have been muted, and why seeing ourselves in stories matters.
  • Identity & Belonging — The ways challenged books tackle race, gender, sexuality, and class.
