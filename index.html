📖 Banned Book Club

A student reading group exploring forbidden stories

📚 Reading List

  • The Great Gatsby F. Scott Fitzgerald
  • 1984 George Orwell
  • To Kill a Mockingbird Harper Lee
  • One Hundred Years of Solitude Gabriel García Márquez

✨ Welcome

We are a group of students who meet weekly to read and discuss books that have been challenged, restricted, or removed from libraries and classrooms. Each session we dive into the themes that make these books targets, talk about why censorship matters, and share what the stories give us.

🌿 Core Themes

  • Censorship & Freedom — How books become “banned” and what we lose when stories are silenced.
  • Diversity & Representation — Voices that have been muted, and why seeing ourselves in stories matters.
  • Identity & Belonging — The ways challenged books tackle race, gender, sexuality, and class.
Join the conversation

Meetings are held every Thursday at 4 PM in the student lounge. All are welcome — no prior reading required. Just bring curiosity and an open mind.
